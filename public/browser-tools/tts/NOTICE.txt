Browser Tools local text-to-speech

The application uses Transformers.js 4.3.2 (Hugging Face, Apache-2.0):
https://github.com/huggingface/transformers.js
https://github.com/huggingface/transformers.js/blob/main/LICENSE

ONNX Runtime Web (Microsoft, MIT) is used for browser-local inference:
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/main/LICENSE

The following Microsoft SpeechT5 models are downloaded on demand from
Hugging Face, not bundled in the application:
- Xenova/speecht5_tts, revision 1723781b8ce2d02f0400c8337be04ae8ee3d6d56,
  q8 encoder and merged decoder (ONNX conversion of microsoft/speecht5_tts).
- Xenova/speecht5_hifigan, revision cf980c3610d7b7f20919960031066ef7905737bd,
  full-precision vocoder (ONNX conversion of microsoft/speecht5_hifigan).

Model source and license declarations:
https://huggingface.co/microsoft/speecht5_tts
https://huggingface.co/microsoft/speecht5_hifigan
https://github.com/microsoft/SpeechT5/blob/main/LICENSE

speaker.npy is the unmodified 512-element speaker embedding
spkrec-xvect/cmu_us_slt_arctic-wav-arctic_a0001.npy from the MIT-licensed
Matthijs/cmu-arctic-xvectors dataset, extracted from spkrec-xvect.zip:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/Matthijs/cmu-arctic-xvectors
Source author: Matthijs Hollemans.
The SLT speaker embedding was extracted from CMU ARCTIC, produced at Carnegie
Mellon University. It is an anonymous preset, not voice cloning or a recording.
CMU ARCTIC attribution and source: http://www.festvox.org/cmu_arctic/
